# Data Sources

This document lists the planned data sources for the **Energy Insights – My Energy Profile** project.  
All datasets are publicly available and subject to the licensing terms of the respective providers.

---

## Energy Consumption & Production

### Our World in Data (OWID)
- Energy production and consumption
- Energy mix and electricity mix
- Renewable energy data

Sources:
- https://ourworldindata.org/energy-production-consumption
- https://ourworldindata.org/energy-mix
- https://ourworldindata.org/electricity-mix
- https://ourworldindata.org/renewable-energy
- https://github.com/owid/energy-data

---

## Electricity Consumption & Prices

### World Bank
- Electricity consumption per capita
- Electricity prices by country

Sources:
- https://databank.worldbank.org/reports.aspx?source=2&series=EG.USE.ELEC.KH.PC
- https://databank.worldbank.org/embed/Electric-Prices-by-Country/id/7b12e700

### Global Petrol Prices
- Household electricity prices

Source:
- https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/electricity_prices/
