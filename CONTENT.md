#Project Concept – Energy Insights

--

##Vision
**Energy Insights – My Energy Profile** aims to help users understand their personal energy consumption by placing it into a national and global context. 
The project connects individual behavior with larger energy systems, making abstract energy and climate data tangible and relatable.

--

##Core Idea
Users configure personal parameters such as country, household size, living situation, age, and heating type. Based on these inputs, the application contextualizes estimated energy consumption using public datasets and visual comparisons.
The focus lies on:
- Personal relevance instead of abstract statistics
- Comparability across countries and regions
- Transparency of data sources and assumptions

--

##Motivation
Energy data is often fragmented, technical, and difficult to interpret. By translating this data into interactive visual narratives, 
the project encourages reflection on personal consumption patterns and highlights global inequalities and transition challenges.

-- 

##Target Audience
- Individuals interested in sustainability and energy
- Students and educators
- Users with no technical background in energy systems

--

##Guiding Principles
- Accessibility over technical complexity
- Visual clarity and intuitive interaction
- Data-driven storytelling
- Modular extensibility for future features
